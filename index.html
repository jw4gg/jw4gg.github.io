Data, systems,
and a few nodes.

Professionally, I work in data management — wrangling pipelines, maintaining integrity, and making information actually useful. My background is in Information Technology, and Perl and Python are the tools that have stuck around the longest.

Outside of work I run a homelab that's grown considerably past "just a server." Proxmox for virtualization, TrueNAS for storage, Portainer for keeping the containers honest. A mix of private and public services, some containerized, some not — built for utility rather than show.

Somewhere in there, a Bitcoin node hums along. Some Ethereum experience too. Nothing flashy — just a preference for running my own infrastructure and understanding what's actually happening on the wire.
