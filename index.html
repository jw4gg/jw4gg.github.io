Short version

I work in data management. My background is IT and the tools I keep reaching for are Perl, Python, and XML. Most days that means moving structured data between systems and making sure nothing breaks on the way through.

At home I run Proxmox on a single box. A few VMs, a stack of containers, TrueNAS for the disks, Portainer for everything that lives in Docker. Some services are public. Most are just for me.

A Bitcoin full node runs here, plus a small self-hosted pool I solo-mine to. I also trade some BTC and ETH on the side. The whole setup is mostly about adding one more node to the distributed network.
