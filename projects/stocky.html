Stocky.

A Discord bot in Python that turns a ticker symbol into a single, useful message: live quote, chart, news, all in one place.

#What it does

You give it a ticker. It gives you back:

  • A real-time quote from Alpha Vantage.
  • A price chart drawn with yFinance data.
  • Recent headlines for the symbol from Finnhub.

All of it in a single Discord message. The chart timeframe (1D, 5D, 1M, and longer) is switched with buttons attached to the message itself, so you don't have to keep re-asking the bot.

#Why I wrote it

I wanted one place to glance at a ticker without bouncing between three different apps. The Discord part is convenient because that's where I already hang out, and Discord's button components are exactly the right shape for "show me the same thing on a different timeframe."

#Architecture

Python, using discord.py for the bot side. Three data sources stitched together:

  • Alpha Vantage for live quotes.
  • yFinance for historical price data the charts get drawn from.
  • Finnhub for news headlines.

Charts are rendered server-side and attached to the response. The buttons re-invoke the chart logic with a different range and edit the original message rather than spamming new ones.

#Status

Working and in use. The most recent round of updates brought it up to Discord's newer interaction patterns so it stops getting yelled at by the deprecation warnings.
